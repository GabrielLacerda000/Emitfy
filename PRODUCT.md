# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brazilian freelancers and small business owners who issue invoices to clients and need to get paid faster. Primary context: end-of-project, often on a laptop, wanting to send an invoice and move on. Secondary context: periodic check-ins on payment status and outstanding amounts. Portuguese-speaking first; bilingual (PT-BR / EN) throughout.

## Product Purpose

Emitfy is a professional invoicing platform. Users create invoices, manage clients, track payment status, and collect payments via Pix (Brazilian instant payment). Success means a user sends their first invoice within 5 minutes of signing up and gets paid without leaving the platform.

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Purposeful. Human. Confident and fast, like a professional tool that respects the user's time. Warm enough that a solo freelancer doesn't feel like they're filing a tax return.

Reference: Lemon Squeezy — modern fintech SaaS with personality; businesslike but not cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-cream: white cards everywhere, teal accent, rounded-everything, indistinguishable from 1000 other tools.
- Ultra-minimal gray-on-white (Notion/Linear clones): cold, uninviting, feels like a side project.
- QuickBooks: dense, dated, enterprise-flavored. Users are freelancers, not accountants.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed signals trust.** Snappy interactions and instant feedback tell a user their money data is in good hands. Never let the UI feel slow or uncertain.
2. **Confidence without formality.** Businesslike enough to trust with finances; approachable enough that a first-time freelancer isn't intimidated. No jargon, no enterprise chrome.
3. **Data earns its space.** Every chart, stat card, and table must justify its presence. No dashboard theater — show only what drives a decision or action.
4. **Pix-native, globally crafted.** The platform is built for Brazil first (Pix, PT-BR, BRL) but holds itself to international fintech design standards.
5. **Clarity over decoration.** The interface should feel like a sharp tool, not a designed artifact. If removing an element makes the page clearer, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full dark/light mode support. Reduced-motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color-blind-safe status semantics (never rely on color alone for invoice status).
